# Maintainers

Ian Hundere ([@ianhundere](https://github.com/ianhundere)) is the sole
maintainer of this experimental repository. Reviews are read-only quality
input, not a second trusted human approval. This project makes no support SLA,
production-readiness, or inherited SLSA qualification claim.

Release defects are corrected with a new prerelease. Existing source, tags,
releases, and assets are not replaced or rewritten.
